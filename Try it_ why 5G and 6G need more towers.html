Experiment 2: follow one message

You tap “send” on your phone. Press Next to see where it goes.

What this shows, and what it doesn’t

It shows

Higher-frequency signals fade faster with distance, so networks that use them need many more, smaller towers. Walls make this worse.

It does not show

Real coverage or speed. Real signals also depend on trees, rain, hills and buildings. The wall losses and the “one bar” limit here are simple assumptions for teaching.

Method: the free-space path-loss formula, loss (dB) = 20·log10(distance in km) + 20·log10(frequency in MHz) + 32.44, with an assumed link budget of 120 dB. Frequency bands follow ITU-R Recommendation M.2160 (2023) and Dahlman et al. (2020). Full references are in the slide deck.
